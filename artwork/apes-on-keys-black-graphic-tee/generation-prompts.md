The artwork and mockup were generated using the built-in image-generation tool from the supplied reference. The requests below describe intended output; measured results are in the README and catalog source record. The output was 1024 × 1536 pixels, despite the request for a larger print master.

## Selected artwork prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: high-resolution apparel print master. Edit target: attached character reference sheet. Extract ONLY the large front-facing full-body ape at the left into one standalone centered cutout. Remove the background and all three smaller right-side views completely. Preserve the main character exactly: surprised face, detailed black fur, red hoodie, red white A-OK cap, red black headphones, hands in pockets, dark pants, red/white sneakers, proportions and 3D textures. Preserve exact legible cap text "A-OK" and hoodie text in three lines "APES" / "ON" / "KEYS". Keep the shoe details as supplied. True transparent alpha background, no white or black rectangle, no shadow beyond subject, no glow, no halo, no additional lettering or figures. Entire body from cap button to soles must be visible. Clean antialiased silhouette, centered with about 4 percent transparent margins, portrait composition. Produce the largest available resolution, ideally at least 3600 pixels tall, suitable for a detailed garment print. This is an extraction, keep the original identity and design.

Place the supplied full-body ape artwork as a centered front-chest garment print, from about 3 inches below collar to mid torso, approximately 6 inches wide by 9 inches tall on an adult medium tee. Faithfully preserve ape face, red hoodie, headphones, cap, footwear, exact lettering "A-OK" and "APES ON KEYS". Graphic must look printed into the black fabric with subtle texture and natural folds, not a separate 3D object. Premium clean commercial studio lighting, realistic black fabric, symmetrical presentation. No model, hanger, props, external text, tags, watermark, extra logos, badges, or decorative graphics.
