# Astra round five — design review and acceptance

Built September 23, 2026. Exactly three new concepts: **24 Print Room**, **25 The Human Edit**, **26 Off Model**. They appear in `/concepts` under **Codex · Astra · Round 5**. All 23 already-published concepts remain available. These Astra concepts were numbered 21–23 in the original brief, and were renumbered 24–26 at publication to preserve the separate Claude Code trio that landed first. The supplied briefs and prior critique are attributed to Astra; these implementations are by Astra (GPT-6) in Codex. No claim is made that three separate implementation agents authored them.

**Recommend Print Room as the production design direction.** Its compact title, actual worn garment, three priced pieces and restrained captions connect the artwork to clothes. This is a design recommendation, not approval to replace the production storefront and not evidence of higher conversion.

**Strongest counterargument:** The Human Edit gets shoppers into the full collection faster. Print Room repeats three featured pieces in the collection, adds scroll, and could drift toward a stationery identity if future iterations replace worn garments with isolated art plates. Keep “Art. On clothes.”, garment categories, visible prices and a worn lead photograph; measure whether that composition improves desire enough to justify the extra browsing distance.

## Comparison

Scores are informed design judgments on a 0–10 scale, not shopper measurements. Weighting: **40% purchase usability, 30% brand voice, 20% distinctive/coherent art direction, 10% responsive execution/accessibility/performance**. Visual direction is assessed independently of prototype completeness. Earlier visual ideas are not penalized for lacking a newer runtime; the interaction evidence is recorded separately below. No speed, conversion or revenue effect is inferred from these scores.

| Concept | Purchase 40% | Voice 30% | Art direction 20% | Execution 10% | Weighted /10 | Main tradeoff |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 16 Human Standard | 8.0 | 8.8 | 8.5 | 7.5 | **8.29** | Strong, unmistakable clothing brand; mobile cover delays the named, priced piece and bag edits reset focus. |
| 20 The Living Index | 8.6 | 7.4 | 7.6 | 7.8 | **8.00** | Efficient collection; density controls and a premature generator invitation compete with a thin editorial identity. |
| 24 Print Room | 8.7 | 9.0 | 9.0 | 8.6 | **8.84** | Best art/clothing synthesis; repeated featured pieces and print-sheet language risk unnecessary distance and a paper-goods association. |
| 25 The Human Edit | 9.3 | 8.3 | 8.0 | 8.8 | **8.69** | Best browsing clarity; specific notes add character, but much of the structure remains familiar catalog design. |
| 26 Off Model | 8.5 | 8.4 | 8.7 | 8.3 | **8.51** | Fashion-led and clearly shoppable; mixing lifestyle photos with catalog mockups limits campaign coherence. |

- **24 — Does it sell clothes or stationery?** The opening worn Puppet Master photograph, product categories, prices and options make clothing explicit. Inspection shows actual labeled alternate garment photographs, not invented print masters. The rules and numbering remain supporting details. The risk returns if typography or isolated artwork takes over.
- **25 — Does curation establish a brand or decorate a catalog?** Notes identify headphones, embroidery, red lenses, drums and the puppeteer graphic. Paired selections connect Hallucination Club to Ape Logo and Puppet Master to March of the Agents. These are useful, specific connections; the core presentation is deliberately conventional and less immediately ownable.
- **26 — Can available imagery sustain a clothing label?** The diptych gives the existing lifestyle image room and pairs it with a real alternate catalog photograph. Named selectors synchronize imagery, price and purchase target; no invented campaign shoot or night filter is used. The inconsistent photographic sources are still visible, so this direction has the largest dependence on future photography.

Both 16 and 20 completed the identical task sequence at desktop and phone sizes. Their focus reset is a verified interaction weakness, not an assumed visual failure. The new concepts keep Add focused and show inline confirmation; opening the bag then takes one additional action versus the old automatic drawer opening. That choice preserves shopping context but is not automatically faster.

## Acceptance and reproducibility

There was no existing prototype test harness. These additive, reversible visual concepts use browser acceptance coverage as the primary proof rather than unit tests that mirror DOM construction. Source/catalog and preservation assertions complement real control activation.

`verify.cjs` runs identical core tasks for **16, 20, 24, 25 and 26 at 1440px and 390px**: find Hallucination Club and $45; reject an unavailable option; select Green/M; add; increase/decrease; remove; inspect art; open sample studio; return to shopping; filter categories. Mobile runs also exercise navigation. New-concept checks cover alternate garment photos, nested-dialog return focus, missing sizes, photo/variant independence, live feedback and Off Model target synchronization.

Six additional scenarios cover **24–26 at 320px and 768px with reduced motion and touch enabled**: no horizontal overflow or animation, empty bag, standard variant purchase, minimum quantity feedback, modal tab containment and bag fit. Two further scenarios verify all four editorial pair links and Off Model under delayed, racing and failed image loads. Photos are decoded before the name, price and purchase target change; a failed load preserves the previous selection. Native button hover/focus/pressed/selected/disabled styles are supplied; keyboard Enter and Escape are exercised. No commerce writes or JavaScript runtime errors occurred in the normal standalone flows. All **18 scenarios passed**; intentional network failure is confined to the failure test.

The expanded suite caught and verified the fix for an Escape-to-next-control race: focus restoration now happens synchronously, so a delayed close event cannot steal focus after the shopper has moved on. The simplification pass applied six small improvements to data reuse, clear initialization/filtering, price formatting and reduced-motion geometry work, without changing the intended presentation.

`verify-gallery.cjs` checks the production `/concepts` route: 26 total entries; exactly three under Astra round five; navigation into 16 and 20; fullscreen entry, comparison and exit; mobile preview width and phone layout.

A separate local source review checked the final dialog, bag, availability, image-race and gallery behavior.
