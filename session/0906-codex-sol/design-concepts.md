# New Design Concepts (Codex + Sol 5.6 pass)

Five additional homepage prototypes in `public/design-concepts/` (11–15). This pass uses the two earlier explorations as constraints: keep the shop product-first, preserve the theorem/game/gallery, design mobile navigation from the start, and avoid repeating the ten existing visual neighborhoods.

## 11 — Museum Store

White-cube museum retail with accession labels, object cards, and gallery wall text. It turns products into collectible cultural artifacts without becoming another fashion editorial.

**Tags:** CURATORIAL · OBJECTS · PREMIUM  
**Best for:** Premium positioning, product photography, and connecting commerce directly to the generative gallery.  
**Risk:** Could feel aloof; copy and price visibility need to keep it approachable.

## 12 — Field Guide

An expedition manual for synthetic wildlife: graph paper, specimen numbers, olive canvas, orange safety marks, and gear-like product cards.

**Tags:** OUTDOOR · TACTILE · FIELD NOTES  
**Best for:** Making the brand feel lived-in and human while keeping a strong system for category and PDP pages.  
**Risk:** The utilitarian metaphor can overpower fashion if photography stays too clinical.

## 13 — Transit Authority

Public wayfinding as storefront. Colored routes become categories; products are destinations; gallery, theorem, and game are transfer stations.

**Tags:** WAYFINDING · CIVIC · COLOR  
**Best for:** Clear navigation, bold brand recognition, and a scalable information system.  
**Risk:** Too many route colors can fragment the existing palette.

## 14 — Receipt Machine

The homepage as a giant thermal receipt: line items, SKU jokes, totals, barcode rhythm, and transaction-native calls to action.

**Tags:** COMMERCE · THERMAL · MONO  
**Best for:** A retail-native joke that keeps pricing and buying extremely legible.  
**Risk:** Monospace density can become visually flat; product color must carry the page.

## 15 — Soft Machine

Optimistic AI culture instead of dystopian tech: biomorphic fields, warm colors, rounded product vessels, and gentler writing.

**Tags:** ORGANIC · OPTIMISTIC · COLORFUL  
**Best for:** Broadening beyond dev culture without sanding off the brand’s strangeness.  
**Risk:** Could drift toward generic wellness unless the monkey theorem and sharper product art stay prominent.

## Recommendation

The strongest strategic pair is **11 Museum Store as the system + 15 Soft Machine as the emotional tone**. Museum Store gives the catalog, PDP, gallery, and navigation a durable structure; Soft Machine prevents that structure from becoming cold. If conversion clarity is the only criterion, 14 Receipt Machine is the cleanest wildcard.
