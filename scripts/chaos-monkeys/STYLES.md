# A-OK Chaos Monkeys: style library

Each draft is a tee or hoodie graphic in one of the styles below. The tool picks a different style for every draft and leans toward the styles the person who picks has kept (see `chaos feedback`). Claude writes the brief inside the style, and GPT-6-Astra draws it.

Homage is part of streetwear, and it is welcome here as homage to a *format*: the box logo, the monogram canvas, the bootleg tee, the varsity arc, the warning label. Never copy another brand's name, wordmark, logo, character, or signature pattern. The words are always A-OK's own, and BRAND.md still governs the character, the palette, and the rules.

Each style is a `## id · Name` heading followed by `engine:` (`astra`, `hybrid specimen`, or `hybrid form`), `text:` (how many short printed strings it allows, beyond the cap and hoodie lettering), an optional `weight:` (relative chance of being picked, default 1), and a description that both models read. Add, remove, or rewrite styles freely.

## box-logo · Box logo
engine: astra
text: 1
The streetwear box logo: one or two words in heavy white italic grotesque, set tight inside a solid signal-red rectangle. The box is the whole design, big and centred. The ape interacts with it: peeks over it, holds it up like a protest sign, is stuck inside it, or props it up like a shelf.

## bootleg · 90s bootleg tee
engine: astra
text: 2
Homage to 90s bootleg rap and wrestling tees: three or four portraits of the ape in different dramatic poses collaged around one big title in brush script or blackletter, with stars, sparkles and heavy outlines. Loud, crowded, affectionate. Render the airbrush look as posterized flat inks, never glossy.

## tour-tee · Tour tee
engine: astra
text: 2
Front of a vintage rock tour tee: an ornate arched name across the top, a dramatic illustration of the ape as the headliner (on stage, crowd-surfing, under one spotlight), and a small banner line underneath.

## varsity · Varsity
engine: astra
text: 2
Collegiate athletics: an arched slab-serif title with a drop-shadow outline, a big varsity numeral that is itself the joke (404, 429, 0.7), and the ape as a felt chenille patch mascot. Letterman-jacket chest energy.

## racing · Racing patches
engine: astra
text: 1
Motorsport jacket: the ape in a racing suit with his cap and headphones, ringed by invented sponsor-style patches made of icons (a GPU, a cursor, a spinner, a token), checkered flags and speed lines. Patches are icons, not words.

## workwear · Workwear label
engine: astra
text: 2
Heritage workwear: a chain-stitched oval name patch or a woven-label rectangle, with the ape as a tradesman doing an AI chore (mechanic, lineman, night janitor of the context window), drawn like a 1950s service-company mark. Restrained, two inks.

## souvenir · Souvenir badge
engine: astra
text: 2
Roadside and national-park souvenir tee: a round or shield badge with a sunburst landscape, an arched name across the top and a small line below. An AI concept is treated as a tourist destination, with the ape as the ranger or the attraction.

## skate-zine · Skate zine
engine: astra
text: 1
80s skate and punk zine graphic: cut-and-paste collage, photocopy grit, xerox halftone, spiky hand lettering, the ape mid-trick or wiping out over an AI prop. Raw and black-heavy with one red accent.

## ape-camo · Ape camo
engine: astra
text: 1
Ura-Harajuku camouflage homage: an all-over camo whose blobs are built from A-OK ape-head silhouettes in red, black, cream and tan, with one solid tag or patch on top where the ape sits. This is A-OK's own camo, never another brand's.

## monogram · Monogram canvas
engine: astra
text: 0
Luxury monogram homage: a repeating canvas of an interlocked A-OK monogram, tiny ape heads and AI glyphs (a cursor, a token, a spinner), tonal cream on tan or red on black, with one centred leather-tag patch where the ape sits, looking stunned to be this expensive. The only letters are the A-OK monogram.

## advisory · Advisory label
engine: astra
text: 2
The music warning-label idiom turned on AI output: a stark black-and-white rectangle with bold condensed words (for example a top line and a big bottom line, like UNVERIFIED / OUTPUT). The ape appears small beside or peeking over the label. Big, blunt, mostly type.

## woodcut · Woodcut
engine: astra
text: 1
Old woodcut or linocut engraving: dense hatching, carved white lines, a scroll banner with one word, and the ape as the figure in an allegorical almanac scene, a medieval marvel of the machine age.

## riso · Risograph
engine: astra
text: 1
Two-colour risograph: red and black overprinting with slight misregistration, grainy fills, bold simple shapes and small type. Contemporary indie print-shop look.

## manual · Instruction manual
engine: astra
text: 1
Appliance-manual technical drawing: the ape in an exploded or cutaway view with numbered callouts (numbers only), dashed arrows and one figure label such as FIG. 1. Clean line art with one red highlight.

## flash · Tattoo flash
engine: astra
text: 1
American traditional tattoo flash: bold outlines, limited fills, a banner scroll with one or two words, roses, stars and lightning, and the ape as the centrepiece. No weapons and no skulls.

## crest · Club crest
engine: astra
text: 2
A football-club or rowing-club crest: shield, laurels, a founding year and a short invented club name, with the ape rampant like a heraldic beast holding an AI emblem.

## rubber-hose · Rubber-hose cartoon
engine: astra
text: 1
1930s rubber-hose animation: the ape with bendy limbs and white gloves, mid-bounce, in a title-card frame with one word in bouncy period lettering. He keeps his cap, headphones and round O mouth.

## pixel · Pixel sprite
engine: astra
text: 1
8-bit game: the ape as a chunky pixel-art sprite in one game moment (a health bar, a power-up, a level-up), with hard pixel edges and only the brand inks.

## swiss · Swiss grid
engine: astra
text: 1
Swiss International Style: a strict grid, the ape simplified into circles and rectangles, one word in a clean grotesque, and a lot of negative space.

## pattern · All-over pattern
engine: astra
text: 0
A repeating all-over print: small ape vignettes doing variations on one action, tiled with AI glyphs. No words at all.

## chest-hit · Small chest hit
engine: astra
text: 1
A minimal left-chest graphic: one crisp ape icon doing one thing, with at most one tiny word beside it. The restraint is the point. Draw it large enough in the preview to judge the detail.

## comic · Comic panel
engine: astra
text: 1
One panel of a vintage comic book: Ben-Day dots, a dramatic angle, the ape mid-reaction, and one speech or thought balloon with a short line.

## specimen · Specimen poster
engine: hybrid specimen
text: 2
The house poster: the title in giant condensed type behind the ape and the slogan in a bar along the bottom. Prints the title and the slogan.

## form · Official form
engine: hybrid form
text: 2
The house form: the ape as a die-cut sticker on an official-looking form, the title as a red rubber stamp and the slogan as the footer. Prints the title, the slogan and the form rows.
