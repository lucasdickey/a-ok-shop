The new base was reconstructed locally from the original figure, then opened in Blender. These clay renders show the actual mesh without color textures. The raw reconstruction is preserved in the Blender file.

New actual mesh — unpainted

Front orthographic view. Cap-to-sole spans are aligned approximately; the original artwork has a different viewing angle.

Changed approach

  • Continuous face, brow, and muzzle geometry.
  • Folded hood with a V at the neck.
  • Loose sleeves leading into pocketed hands.
  • Gathered trouser fabric and a more coherent silhouette.
  • Localized jacket-width correction and removal of generated webbing between the feet.

Still unfinished

  • Eyes, nose, lips, cheeks, and chin need closer likeness work.
  • Sneakers need recognizable soles, panels, tongues, and laces.
  • Branding, zipper detail, ribbing, and fur texture need reconstruction.
  • Color/paint regions and the attachment are not yet supplied.
  • No fabrication validation or print approval applies to this study.
